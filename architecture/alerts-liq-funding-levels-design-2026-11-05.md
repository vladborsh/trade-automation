**C1. `market_alerts` columns:**
- id, type, event, symbol, direction (nullable), at (the event's own moment, the link's `at`), tf;
- details jsonb, validated per type and event by contracts;
- params jsonb (as used), source, build;
- confirmed_at, fired_at, late, recipients;
- `dedupe_key text`, with **unique (source, type, dedupe_key)**. Each type defines its key below, so "once" is a constraint, not a promise.

**C2. Late.** A loop that wakes after downtime journals what it missed with `late = true` and does not send it, as in V10. Each type gives its own staleness bound below.

**C3. Texts.**
- Built by the bot from keys, ru and en.
- Tickers, venues and level names (PWH, PMH …) stay English; sentences are translated.
- No forecasts anywhere (D20).
- Every message ends with the instrument link.

**C4. Before launch, per type:** a history run over our stored data, with the same steps for the owner as IB-111:
- the alert count a week at the defaults and at ½× and 2×;
- 5–10 examples on the instrument page, pictures in the report;
- a short report in plain Russian.

The owner then sets thresholds on `/admin/alerts`. Sending stays off until the owner switches that type on.

**C5. No hit rates.** §6 of the OI design applies to every type: "worked" and the baseline must be decided before any rate is written. Controls are collected now so the baseline exists then.

## 2. Large liquidations (first)

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | **A sliding 5-minute window, evaluated every 30 s,** per coin and per liquidated side.<br>• At each tick the loop reads that side's liquidations of the last 5 min + 30 s from the tape, all three venues, and finds the **first moment *t* at which the sum over `(t − 5 min, t]` reached the coin's threshold**. That moment is the alert's `at`.<br>• **There are no buckets, so there is no bucket edge.** A cascade that straddles a minute boundary is one window. | Fixed 5-minute buckets would split a $6M cascade into $3M + $3M and miss a $5M threshold twice. A sliding window cannot. The exact crossing moment makes the link land on the cascade, not on the tick. |
| L2 | **Anti-spam per (coin, side):**<br>• after an alert, the coin and side are quiet for **30 min from `at`**;<br>• in that time, the **running sum from the alert window's start** (`at − 5 min`) is tracked. When it reaches **2 × the threshold**, one `continuing` message is sent: «продолжается: уже $X». At most one per quiet period;<br>• after 30 min, detection is normal again, so a cascade still going gets a fresh alert.<br>`dedupe_key`: `liq:{symbol}:{side}:{window start}` for the alert, `liq-cont:{symbol}:{side}:{alert id}` for the continuation. | **Amended 2026-11-05 (owner, §2a): messages are per market event, L10–L15. The 30-min quiet per (coin, side) stays; the per-coin 2× continuation is replaced by L13.** As ruled. "Running sum since the window start" is the only reading of "reaches 2× within those 30 minutes" in which the continuation reports how big the event has become. |
| L3 | **Venues are summed, and the message shows the split.** Message:<br>• «Крупные ликвидации **лонгов** BTC: **$7.4M** за 5 мин»;<br>• the next line: «Binance $3.1M · Bybit $2.9M · OKX $1.4M»;<br>• then: «{count} ликвидаций, крупнейшая $1.2M (Bybit)».<br>Binance publishes at most one liquidation per second per symbol (D6), so its figure is a floor; the message says so in a short footnote the first time per day. **A venue whose stream was silent in the window** (collector heartbeats) is named: «нет данных с OKX». It is never shown as $0. | The owner asked for the split, the count and the largest. Naming a silent venue keeps an undercount from looking like a quiet venue (IB-BUG-011). |
| L4 | **Default thresholds** (5 min, all venues): BTC $5M, ETH $3M, SOL $1M, BNB $300k, TRX $300k. Editable per coin and on/off per coin in `/admin/alerts`. A coin added to `alert_symbols` without a threshold is **off** until one is entered. | No default fits an arbitrary coin, and a wrong default is spam or silence. |
| L5 | **Coverage of a newly listed coin.** Today Binance stores every USDT-linear `!forceOrder` (all market), but **Bybit subscribes per symbol and OKX filters to the universe**. So TRX, which is listed but not in the universe, has Binance only.<br>• The liquidation collectors **track `universe ∪ alert_symbols(enabled)`**, re-read every 5 min, as V3 does for OI. Bybit subscribes to new topics without a reconnect, and OKX widens its filter.<br>• Venue symbols come from the base: `{BASE}USDT` (Binance, Bybit), `{BASE}-USDT-SWAP` (OKX). This is a pure mapper with a spec, used by both the collectors and the loop.<br>• Until a venue has collected a coin for 5 min, the message names it as not collected. | "A coin added there auto-joins the data the alert needs" (owner). The tape cannot be backfilled: no venue serves liquidation history. |
| L6 | **Late:** an `at` older than 5 min at evaluation (after downtime) is journalled `late`, not sent. | A cascade reported 20 min late is history, not an alert. |
| L7 | **Cost:**<br>• **venue weight 0**: the tape is ours;<br>• **DB:** 2 reads a minute of a ≤ 5.5-min range on the current partition. They use the `(venue, symbol, ts, …)` unique index, with symbols mapped as in L5. Coins in a quiet period add one read of ≤ 35 min each;<br>• **memory:** the window's rows only, a few thousand at the worst minutes, ≈ 50 B each, well under 1 MB;<br>• price paths: 8 weight per alert (V24). | Small next to IB-096's cap. The report measures it on a slot. |
| L8 | **Controls and near-misses** (`market_alert_controls`):<br>• **`random`**: 2 random 5-minute windows per coin and day, hash-seeded as V25, with their sum recorded. This answers "is a big window different from any window?";<br>• **`near_miss`**: per coin, side and UTC hour, the **largest** 5-minute sum when it reached **≥ 0.5 ×** the threshold and fired no alert, with `ratio = sum / threshold`. A threshold change can then be re-scored on stored rows.<br>Paths are measured for both, direction-free (`direction` null, stop null), as for V25's random. | The owner will tune thresholds. Near-misses below them are the data for it. One per hour bounds the volume to ≤ 24 × 2 × 5 = 240 a day in the worst case, a few MB a year. |
| L9 | **History run (C4).** Replay the tape from its first complete day: per coin, the alert count a week at the defaults, ½× and 2×, continuation counts, and 5–10 examples. A window with a venue gap (`collector_gaps`) is still counted but marked. TRX has Binance only before L5. | The owner's gate. The tape is the only source. |

### 2a. Round 2: one message per market event, sent once it settles (owner 2026-11-05)

**Input:** `~/.claude/jobs/0028abca/tmp/reports/coordinator-owner-rulings-4h-and-liq-alerts-2026-11-05.md` §2, after the owner read `dev-3-IB-123-owner.md`.

In the 41-hour IB-123 run, 25 coin alerts made up 10 market events. 18 of 19 continuations came 0–121 s after their alert. The first message usually carried about the threshold, and the real size only came a minute later.

**Owner rulings:**
- Q1: one message per same-side event, listing every coin.
- Q2: send after 1 minute with no new liquidation, but no later than 3 minutes after the first crossing. A «продолжается» goes only if the event runs past that.
- Q3: thresholds unchanged.
- Q4: rerun the same 41 h, then go live for the owner only.

**One task, IB-143. No migration:** the journal already holds what the event needs (`event` is free text, `details` is jsonb validated by contracts).

**Measured on the box tape** (read-only, 2026-11-05, the 25 backfill alerts of IB-123): the first 60 s with no print of that coin and side came a **median 132 s** after the coin's crossing. It came within 180 s for **18 of 25** coins, ranged 6–385 s, and was reached by every coin within 6.5 min. These are per-coin gaps; an event with several coins settles later. So the owner's rule works literally, with "any print, any size". No "small print" threshold is needed. This is one occasion over one stretch of tape, so it is a note, not a rule. The rerun (L15) gives the event-level figures.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| L10 | **The coin crossing stays as it is, but is no longer sent by itself.**<br>• L1's sliding window still finds each coin's first moment `at` at which its 5-min sum reaches its threshold, per coin and side.<br>• L2's **30-minute quiet per (coin, side) from `at`** stays, so one coin cannot open or join a second event inside one cascade.<br>• The crossing is still journalled as `event = 'alert'`, with its own once-key, controls and price path (L8, V24). `details` gains **`eventAt`**, the moment its event opened.<br>• These rows are **never sent**. The message is the event (L11). | The crossing is the research subject: a hit rate per coin still means something. The message is the owner's reading unit. One row for each keeps the statistics unchanged. |
| L11 | **The market event (Q1), per liquidated side, and its grouping window.**<br>• **Opens** at the first crossing `T0` of a side when no event of that side is open.<br>• **Joins:** every other coin crossing on the same side **while the event is open**.<br>• **Members:** the coins that have crossed. Their prints of that side, any size, all three venues, are "the event's liquidations". A coin that never crossed does not count, so a quiet small coin cannot keep an event alive.<br>• **The first message goes at `S`:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– the first moment ≥ `T0` with **60 s without a member print**, i.e. the last print + 60 s ("settled");<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– **at most `T0 + 3 min`** ("cap").<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– A coin crossing before `S` resets the 60 s, because its prints are members from then on.<br>• **Closes:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– at `S` if it settled;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– otherwise at the first 60 s without a member print after `S`;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– **at the latest `T0 + 30 min`** (= L2's quiet, so an event cannot outlive the quiet of its first coin).<br>• **So the grouping window** is: crossings by `S` (≤ 3 min) go into the first message, and crossings until the close (≤ 30 min) go into the continuation. A crossing after the close opens a new event.<br>• **Long and short events are independent.**<br>• Constants in contracts, not admin params, because they are rulings: `LIQ_SETTLE_SEC = 60`, `LIQ_SEND_CAP_SEC = 180`, `LIQ_EVENT_MAX_SEC = LIQ_QUIET_SEC`. | The owner asked for "within a few minutes". Tying the window to the event's own activity, rather than a fixed span, means one cascade is one message however its coins stagger: 14:41 → 14:48 in IB-123 was one move. Counting member prints only is the literal "no new liquidation in the event". |
| L12 | **The first message (Q1, Q2)**, `event = 'event'`, one journal row:<br>• **`symbol`** is the link coin: the member with the largest sum at `S` (as in episodes E6).<br>• **Once-key:** `liq-ev:{side}:{T0}`.<br>• `details`:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– `side`, `eventAt = T0`, `sentAt = S`, `settled: 'quiet' \| 'cap'`;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– per coin `{symbol, crossedAt, windowStartMs, sumUsd, thresholdUsd, byVenue, count, largest, silentVenues}`, where each coin's sum runs **from its own window start (`crossedAt − 5 min`) to `S`**;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– `totalUsd`, `count`, `largest {usd, venue, symbol}`, `binanceFootnote`.<br>• **Text** (C3, keys, ru/en):<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– «Крупные ликвидации **лонгов**: **$21.2M** за {m} мин»;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– one line per coin, largest first: «BTC $12.7M — Binance 7.1 · Bybit 4.0 · OKX 1.6», with L3's «нет данных с OKX» per coin;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– «{count} ликвидаций, крупнейшая $1.2M (BTC, Bybit)»;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– the link to the link coin at its `crossedAt` (`?at=&tf=5m`).<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– `{m}` = from the earliest window start to `S`, rounded up to whole minutes.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– With one member, the text reads like the old single-coin alert. | "The true event size" means each coin's sum through the moment it settled or the cap. Each coin's start is its own window, so a sum never includes a coin's quiet minutes before its cascade. |
| L13 | **The continuation (Q2): at most one, at the close, and only if it adds something.** `event = 'event_continuing'`, once-key `liq-ev-cont:{side}:{T0}`.<br>• **Sent only when** the event hit the **cap** and, between `S` and the close, **a new coin joined** or **a member added at least its own threshold**.<br>• A cascade that only dribbles on past the cap ends silently. Its final size is still journalled.<br>• **Text:** «Ликвидации **лонгов** продолжаются: уже **$X** за {m} мин», then per-coin lines with new coins marked «+BNB», then the link.<br>• `details`: the same shape as L12 at the close, plus `firstMessageId` and `closedBy: 'quiet' \| 'max'`.<br>• **This replaces L2's per-coin 2× continuation.** `runningSum` is no longer used for it. Old `continuing` rows stay in the journal under their name. | "One message at the end, or at most one more" (owner). The bar "at least one threshold more" carries over L2's 2×: a continuation that does not report a second threshold's worth is not news. |
| L14 | **Timing, late, restart.**<br>• **The tick goes from 30 s to 10 s** (`LIQ_TICK_SEC = 10`), so `S` is acted on ≤ 10 s after it, and the cap means ≤ 3 min 10 s, not up to 3 min 30 s. The near-miss and random logic is unchanged (`OVERLAP_MS` follows the tick).<br>• **Cost:** the tick's reads go from ≈ 2 + 2 per chunk every 30 s to the same every 10 s, ≈ 25 DB reads a minute, each over ≤ 5.5 min of one partition, plus one read per open event. Venue weight is still 0.<br>• **Late (L6):** a first message whose `S`, or a continuation whose close, is more than 5 min before `now` is journalled `late` and not sent. A late `T0` makes the whole event late.<br>• **State is the journal:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– open events are the `alert` rows of the last 35 min grouped by `details.eventAt`, minus those with a closing row;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– the `event` / `event_continuing` rows say which phase each is in.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– A restart therefore resumes the event, and the once-keys absorb any duplicate. No new table.<br>• **A print that arrives after `S` was decided** does not reopen the sent message. It counts toward the continuation. | The owner complained about lag on the OI alerts. 10 s costs a few cheap reads. Deriving the state from rows that are already written is what made L2 survive restarts, and it holds here too. |

## 3. Funding (second)

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Inputs, every 5 min:**<br>• the coin's **current predicted rate**, `/fapi/v1/premiumIndex?symbol=`, weight 1 each, so 5 coins = 5 weight per 5 min = 1.0 / min;<br>• its **30-day settled history**, `/fapi/v1/fundingRate`, weight 1, refreshed hourly: 5 / h ≈ 0.08 / min. That history is not stored (S1); the positioning baseline holds it only for universe coins, and the alert list can hold others.<br>**Total ≈ 1.08 weight / min** (corrected 2026-11-06: the first text said ≈ 0.3, an arithmetic slip that counted 5 coins × weight 1 as 1 per 5 min. Slot dev-3 measured 10 on the first check, then 5 per check, one run on 2026-11-06, a note: `reports/dev-3-IB-124.md` §Measured). | Funding is not in our tape (storage ruling S1). The cost is negligible: ≈ 0.05 % of 2 400. |
| F2 | **Normalised to 8 h.** Some coins settle every 4 h or 1 h. The interval is read from the spacing of the last settlements (no extra call), and the rate is compared as `rate × 8 h / interval`. The message prints the venue's own rate and interval. | The owner's floor is "±0.03 % per 8 h". A 4 h coin at 0.02 % pays as much as an 8 h coin at 0.04 %. |
| F3 | **"Extreme" = both:**<br>• beyond the coin's own **p95 / p5 of its 30-day settled rates**, normalised. This is the same sample and the same quantile function as rule R5 and the rarity: one shared helper, with a spec that R5 and the alert give the same band for the same sample;<br>• and beyond the **floor**, ±0.03 % / 8 h by default, editable per coin. *(Amended 2026-11-05, market round 3 §1: the default is `FUNDING_FLOOR_PER_8H` in `insights/thresholds.ts` and the check is `fundingExtreme`, both from IB-135, shared with R5. Do not declare a second constant.)*<br>High side: `r ≥ max(p95, +floor)`; low side: `r ≤ min(p5, −floor)`. | As ruled. Sharing the band means the Market card and the alert can never disagree about what "unusual" is. |
| F4 | **Entry event:**<br>• a coin's state per side is `normal → extreme` when the condition holds at a check. One `enter` alert;<br>• it re-arms **only after the condition has been false for 3 consecutive checks (15 min)**, so a rate wobbling on the edge does not alert at every check;<br>• `dedupe_key`: `funding:{symbol}:{side}:{episode start}`. | "One alert per entry, re-armed after return to normal" (owner), with a stated hysteresis. |
| F5 | **"Sustained" = the extreme episode lasted ≥ 24 h** (three 8 h settlements), counting from entry to exit as F4 defines them.<br>• After such an episode ends, the first time the rate's **sign** is the opposite of the episode's, held for 3 checks and within **72 h of the episode's end**, one `flip` alert is sent: «фандинг сменил знак после {h} ч экстремума».<br>• One per episode; `dedupe_key` `funding-flip:{symbol}:{episode start}`. | A flip after a day of crowding is the event the owner means. A flip after an hour is noise, and a flip a week later is unrelated. Both bounds are editable params. |
| F6 | **State survives a restart.** It lives in `alert_funding_state` (symbol, side, zone, since, false_checks, last episode: start, end, sign, flip_sent), **one small row per coin and side**. Predicted funding has no history anywhere, so in-memory state would be lost with nothing to rebuild it from. | Five coins × 2 rows. |
| F7 | **Late:** a check whose rate is more than 15 min old (after downtime) is not alerted; the state is rebuilt from the next check. | Nothing to replay: predicted rates are not historical. |
| F8 | **Controls:** `random`, 2 checks a coin a day with the rate and band recorded; `near_miss`, an entry into p95/p5 that **failed only the floor**, or crossed the floor while inside the band, at most one a coin and side a day. Paths are measured, with direction = the side that pays (longs paying → bearish crowding, stored as is, no forecast). | The floor and the band are the two knobs; near-misses re-score both. |
| F9 | **History run (C4)** on **settled** funding. Predicted history does not exist, so the replay checks each settlement against the previous 30 days. **It is coarser than live (8 h, not 5 min), and the report says so.** Span: 180 days for the counts (weight 1 per 1 000 settlements, negligible). | The only history there is. Counts per week are what the owner needs, and 8 h granularity is enough for them. |

## 4. Price levels (third)

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **One level module, shared.** `prior-levels.ts` moves **from the dashboard into `libs/market-data-core`**, with its spec, and is extended. The dashboard imports it from there in the same commit: a pure move, behaviour unchanged, spec green. A second copy would drift. Rules kept: **complete periods only**, UTC, ISO weeks from Monday 00:00 UTC; no level is better than a wrong one. | The brief offers "own copy or shared"; shared is the one that cannot drift. |
| P2 | **Levels:**<br>• on by default: **PWH / PWL** (previous ISO week), **PMH / PML** (previous calendar month), **weekly open** (the open of the current week's first 1d bar), **monthly open**;<br>• off by default, on per coin in admin: **PDH / PDL**.<br>• Computed from 1d klines, one call per coin per UTC day (limit ≈ 70, weight 1), and recomputed when a period rolls over.<br>• **Each event type can be switched off per level kind** in `/admin/alerts` (`params.events[kind][event]`; on by default wherever the level itself is on), so crossunder of PWH can be off while its breakout stays on (2026-11-05). | As ruled. 1d bars give every level, including opens. |
| P3 | **Events on each 15m close** (the bar closed + 5 s settle):<br>• **breakout up:** `close > L` and the previous 15m close `≤ L`; breakout down mirrors it;<br>• **sweep above:** `high > L`, `close ≤ L` and the previous close `≤ L` (it came from below, poked through, closed back); sweep below mirrors it;<br>• a high exactly at L, or a close exactly at L, is a touch: **no alert**.<br>• **crossunder** of a high level (PWH, PMH, PDH): `close < L`, and the level's **last non-touch 15m close in this period was above L** (P10 keeps it). That is, at least one 15m close above L earlier in the period, and now a close back below: the breakout did not hold. **Crossover** of a low level (PWL, PML, PDL) mirrors it. *(Added 2026-11-05, owner ruling via the coordinator.)*<br>**Which side each level is evaluated from** (made explicit 2026-11-05): a **high** level gets breakout up, sweep above and crossunder; a **low** level gets breakout down, sweep below and crossover. An **open** (weekly or monthly) is a single level from both sides and gets breakout up/down and sweep above/below, **with no crossunder/crossover**: a close back across an open *is* a breakout in the other direction, which P4 now keys by direction.<br>**Precedence:** a sweep needs price to come from the near side (the last non-touch close on L's near side); a crossunder needs it to come from the far side. They therefore cannot both fire on one candle. The one ambiguous shape (previous close exactly at L, an earlier close above L, a wick above and a close below) is a **crossunder**, the more specific fact. | As ruled. "The previous close" gives each event a direction without a tolerance parameter. A crossunder needs ≥ 1 full close beyond first, so it never overlaps a sweep (one candle, wick only). |
| P4 | **Repeats:** at most **one message per (level, event type) per period while the level is in force**, `dedupe_key`: `level:{symbol}:{level kind}:{period start}:{event}`, where event is `breakout_up|breakout_down|sweep_above|sweep_below|crossunder|crossover` *(amended 2026-11-05: the key names the direction)*. For a high or low level, nothing changes, because each event has one direction there. For an **open**, a breakout up and a later breakout back down are reported **once each** per period. A week of chop around PWH gives at most one breakout up and one crossunder.<br>Periods: PDH/PDL a day; PWH/PWL and weekly open a week; PMH/PML and monthly open a month. | As ruled; the key enforces it. |
