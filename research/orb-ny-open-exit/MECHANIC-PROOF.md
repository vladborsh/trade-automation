Conventions recalled: the opening range is the 1-minute high/low over [09:30, 09:35) NY; the first eligible trigger bar is the **5-minute bar opening at the range's end, 09:35 NY**; a bar triggers on its **close** strictly beyond the range, never on a touch; entry is that bar's close; one trade per day, no re-entry; **no arm in this family has a target**; every arm is forced flat at the 16:00 NY minute's close; the exit path is walked on **1-minute** bars with the stop checked first, and a minute that opens through the stop fills at that minute's open.

The three constructions:

- **E0** — range stop, no target, flat 16:00 NY.
- **E1** — range stop, no target, 1R ratchet, flat 16:00 NY.
- **E2** — stop at 0.10 x dATR14, no target, flat 16:00 NY.
