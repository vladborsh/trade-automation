## Selection audit

* Protocol: `VIZ-SPEC.md` §3 — fixed quotas **4 target / 4 stop / 2 time**, deterministic draw from seed 42 upward, first seed whose draw satisfies every §3.5 representativeness constraint wins. **Winning seed: 42.** No trade was hand-picked, vetoed or swapped.
* Full pooled ledger (8 perps, 2022-08-01 → 2026-08-01, `eod` rows dropped): **6787 trades** — target 1727 (25.4%), stop 2341 (34.5%), time 2719 (40.1%). True pooled win rate **44.9%**. The 40/40/20 chart mix therefore over-represents target exits and under-represents time exits relative to the real mix; it is chosen to show all three exit mechanics, not to mirror their frequencies.
* Reproduction gate (`VIZ-SPEC.md` §5, `REPORT.md` §2.3): per-asset net PF 0.97 / 1.03 / 1.11 (min/median/max), median WR 44.8%, pooled trades 6792 — matches the report before any trade was selected.
* Data: the existing 1h pulls (`data.binance.vision` USD-M perpetual monthly klines), copied unchanged from the v2 research worktree. Nothing was re-fetched, and the engine and parameters are untouched.

**Reminder:** `momentum_breakout` nets ≈ **+0.019 R per trade** after 5 bp/side costs. These ten charts illustrate the mechanics of the rule — how the trigger, the stop, the target and the time stop behave — and are **not** a performance claim. A 4-win / 4-loss / 2-timeout sample says nothing about the edge; the report's pooled numbers do.
