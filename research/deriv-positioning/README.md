# research/deriv-positioning — derivatives-positioning signals (OI / taker flow / long-short ratios)

Execution of `DESIGN.md`, the Architect's pre-committed protocol for the first
**non-OHLCV** input this project has tested: Binance's free futures-metrics
archive (open interest, top-trader long/short ratios, taker buy/sell volume
ratio) at 5 m, merged onto 1 h decision bars.

**Read `REPORT.md` for the findings.** Headline: **all three pre-registered
hypotheses are dead and the holdout was never read.**

* **(a) OI-tercile conditioning of `momentum_breakout`** — passed stage 1
  (t = 2.08 / 2.54 / 3.44 at 12 / 24 / 36 h) and passed its 200-draw
  random-subset null, then **failed the §5.3 redundancy control**: the identical
  test with ΔOI swapped for the trailing-4 h-return percentile separates the
  same triggers *better at 5 of 6 horizons*, beats the null at 6 of 6, and is
  positive on 8/8 symbols where ΔOI manages 7/8 at best. Priced through the
  unchanged engine, the pre-registered filter keeps 55 % of trades and 10 % of
  the net PnL — the §3 comparative bar fails on both legs.
* **(b1) crowding squeeze** — failed stage 1 and is significantly **negative**
  (−58.4 bp at 24 h, t = −4.56, 0th percentile of its random-event null).
  Crowded-extreme + adverse break is a fade, not a squeeze.
* **(b2) taker-imbalance continuation** — failed stage 1, also negative
  (−19.8 bp at 24 h, t = −2.86), while its volume shadow is a clean zero.

**The finding, in one line:** Binance's public positioning feeds add no signal
beyond price at 1 h — and *not* because they are price in disguise. Spearman ρ
against their price shadows is 0.12 / 0.04 / 0.003; they are genuinely
independent measurements that carry genuinely less. Holding price fixed and
splitting by ΔOI inside it yields 1 significant cell out of 18, the chance rate.

**One Architect ruling was obtained at stage 0**, before any result existed: the
archive starts the two top-trader ratio columns on 2022-12-01 (all other columns
on 2022-08-01), uniformly for all 8 symbols, which mechanically tripped §1.3's
">3 of 8 symbols dropped → STOP". Ruled a coverage artefact governed by §6's
window-adjustment clause: hypothesis (b1) — and only (b1) — runs on an effective
fit window of 2022-12-16 → 2025-04-01, everything else unchanged. `REPORT.md`
§1.4 has the full text and provenance.

Also recorded so nobody re-derives it: **`data.binance.vision`'s
`futures/um/monthly/metrics/` prefix does not exist** (404 for every symbol and
month). The pull runs entirely on §1's pre-committed daily fall-back — 11,688
files, 1,461/1,461 days present for all 8 symbols.

Imports v1 (`../intraday-mr-smc`: `indicators.py`, `strategies.py`,
`backtest.py`) and v2 (`strategies_v2.py`) unchanged; nothing in either is
modified. `momentum_breakout` runs at v2 defaults and was not re-tuned. The
engine (`backtest.py:run`) and the risk rules (1 %/trade, 2 %-of-day breaker)
are used as-is.

**Data:** 5 m klines are symlinked from `../intraday-mtf-smc/data/`; the raw
metrics pull lives in `data/` and is gitignored (regenerate with
`fetch_metrics.py`, ~25 min). `cache/` is gitignored. `results/` is committed
except the per-trade logs `results/trades_*.csv`, regenerated by `stage3.py`.
See `REPORT.md` §10 for the run order and a file-by-file map.
