Conventions recalled: the opening range is the **00:00–00:30 UTC** 1-minute high/low; a 5-minute bar triggers on its **close** strictly beyond it, never on a touch; the trigger window is **[00:30, 02:30) UTC**; entry is the trigger bar's close; the stop is the opposite side of the range; template 1 targets +1R and is forced flat at **07:55 UTC** (five minutes before the 08:00 UTC funding settlement); the exit path is walked on **1-minute** bars with the stop checked first. Every day of the week is eligible — the sample below deliberately contains weekend days.
