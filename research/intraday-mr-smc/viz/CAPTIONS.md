Every chart shows the order block and/or fair value gap the entry retested (shaded rectangle, blue OB / orange FVG, drawn from the zone's formation bar to the signal bar), the swing level the most recent break of structure took out (dotted grey, with a `BOS`/`CHoCH` diamond on the breaking bar), the signal bar that traded into the zone and closed back across it, the fill / stop / 2R-target lines over the holding period, and the exit.

## Selection audit

* Protocol: `VIZ-SPEC-SMC.md` §3 — fixed quotas **3 target / 5 stop / 2 time**, pre-committed textbook sub-quotas (≥3 textbook stops, ≥2 textbook targets, ≥1 FVG-only trigger) and the §3.7 representativeness constraints, drawn deterministically from seed 42 upward with `numpy.random.default_rng`. First fully-satisfying seed wins. **Winning seed: 43.** No trade was hand-picked, vetoed or swapped; the rejection loop is the only selection mechanism.
* Full pooled ledger (BTC/ETH/SOL, 1h, 2022-08-01 → 2026-08-01, `eod` rows dropped): **7207 trades** — target 1642 (22.8%), stop 3634 (50.4%), time 1931 (26.8%). True pooled win rate **38.1%**. The 3/5/2 chart mix is weighted toward losses to echo that win rate while still showing three full 2R payoffs.
* Trigger mix across the full ledger: FVG 2620 (36.4%), OB 4278 (59.4%), OB+FVG 309 (4.3%).
* Reproduction gate (`VIZ-SPEC-SMC.md` §5, `REPORT.md` §3): per-symbol BTCUSDT n=2408 WR=37.7% PF=0.71, ETHUSDT n=2387 WR=38.0% PF=0.81, SOLUSDT n=2415 WR=38.6% PF=0.91 — reproduces the report's `smc / 1h` rows (2408/37.7%/0.71, 2387/38.0%/0.81, 2415/38.6%/0.91) before any trade was selected.
* Data: the existing 1h pulls (`data.binance.vision` USD-M perpetual monthly klines), copied unchanged from the v2 research worktree per §5. Nothing was re-fetched; the engine, rule-set and parameters (`smc_k=2`, `smc_rr=2.0`, `atr_len=14`, `time_stop=24`, 5 bp/side, 1% risk) are untouched.

## The framing numbers

* **4512 of 7207 trades (62.6%) are `textbook`** — an order-block retest of a break of structure no more than 20 bars old. Their win rate is **35.3%**, against a full-ledger **38.1%** and a 2R break-even line of **33.3%**.
* `edge_probe.py` measured **−0.03 bp** of direction-adjusted forward return over **46,045** non-overlapping `smc` 1h triggers, median t-stat +0.22, zero cells with t > 2 (`REPORT.md` §4).

The pattern is not rare and the entries are not obviously wrong: the textbook setups look exactly like the playbook and resolve barely above the coin-flip their own 2R geometry demands. That is the failure — a clean, legible rule with no forward edge to trade. These ten charts show how that rule behaves bar by bar; they are not a performance claim, and the candidate stays retired.
